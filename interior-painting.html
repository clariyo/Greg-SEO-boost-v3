What the work covers

Interior Painting & Decorating — done in the right order

The preparation is where the difference is made. Filling, sanding, priming and caulking every junction so the topcoat goes on smooth and stays looking right. Whether it's one room, a full house, or a property being readied for sale, the work is methodical, the site is kept tidy, and the finish is clean.

A flawless topcoat only holds up when the work beneath it has been done with the same care. Greg treats preparation as the real job — so the finish is clean, the lines are true, and the paint goes the distance.

  • Walls, ceilings, woodwork, doors and feature rooms
  • Filling, sanding, priming and caulking every junction
  • Single rooms, full-house redecoration, or pre-sale refreshes
  • Floors, furniture and fittings protected throughout
  • A tidy site every day, and a spotless one at the end
