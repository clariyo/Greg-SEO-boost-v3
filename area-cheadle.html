Homes in Cheadle

The Cheadle housing stock, and what it asks of a decorator

Cheadle runs the full range: Victorian and 1930s semis along the older streets, post-war estates towards Cheadle Hulme, and newer builds near the Mersey. Each era brings its own quirks — lath-and-plaster ceilings that need a gentle hand, picture rails and coving worth preserving, and render that weathers differently on every elevation.

Greg works on these houses week in, week out, so the preparation approach is matched to the property rather than pulled off a checklist. That matters most in the older stock, where skipping the filling and sanding stage shows within months.
